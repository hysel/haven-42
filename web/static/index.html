LET'S GET STARTED

Welcome to Haven 42

Haven 42 helps you use AI on your own computer. The recommended setup checks what your computer can run and explains every download before asking permission.

  • Your chats stay in memory and are not saved by Haven 42.
  • Haven 42 does not change drivers, Windows settings, services, or automatic updates.
  • You will see what is needed and approve it before anything is downloaded.

              ELECTRICITY ESTIMATE

              Estimate graphics-card cost

              Estimate only

              Use a measured graphics-card average and the price from your electricity bill. Haven 42 does not detect your location or change your model choice.

              A Haven 42 test profile applies only to the exact card, model, runtime, and workload shown.

              Copy the per-kWh price from your bill. The value and country stay in this browser session.

              Your electricity-bill information stays private. When you enter the price from your bill, Haven 42 uses it only to calculate the estimate on this screen. It does not save, upload, or add that price or your usage values to troubleshooting logs. Closing Haven 42 clears them. Choosing an official average contacts the named statistics service, but never sends your bill price.

              What do these price choices mean?

              Your electricity bill is usually the best choice. Look for a price shown per kilowatt-hour or kWh.

              Average U.S. household price comes from the U.S. Energy Information Administration, a government statistics agency. It is a national or state average, not the exact price from your utility or ZIP code.

              Average European household price comes from Eurostat, the European Union's statistics office. It is a country average and may differ from your electricity plan.

              This estimates graphics-card electricity only. It excludes the CPU, memory, storage, cooling, display, and power-supply losses, so it is not a prediction of your utility bill.
